## Text
- Arabic matn [added]: أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم أُتِيَ بِجَنَازَةٍ لِيُصَلِّيَ عَلَيْهَا، فَقَالَ: «هَلْ عَلَيْهِ مِنْ دَيْنٍ؟» قَالُوا: لاَ. فَصَلَّى عَلَيْهِ، ثُمَّ أُتِيَ بِجَنَازَةٍ أُخْرَى، فَقَالَ: «هَلْ عَلَيْهِ مِنْ دَيْنٍ؟» قَالُوا: نَعَمْ. قَالَ: «صَلُّوا عَلَى صَاحِبِكُمْ». قَالَ أَبُو قَتَادَةَ: عَلَيَّ دَيْنُهُ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ. فَصَلَّى عَلَيْهِ
- English gist: The Prophet ﷺ prayed over a debt-free deceased but told the people to pray themselves over an indebted one, until Abū Qatādah undertook the debt, whereupon he prayed over him.
- Kitab / bab: Book of Guarantees (Kafālah), chapter "Whoever guarantees a debt on behalf of a deceased person has no right to retract".

## Chain (as in al-Bukhari, compiler down to the Prophet ﷺ)
1. al-Bukhārī | "narrated to us" (حدثنا) → Abū ʿĀṣim | shaykh known by kunya and laqab (Abū ʿĀṣim al-Nabīl = al-Ḍaḥḥāk ibn Makhlad)
2. Abū ʿĀṣim | "from" (عن) → Yazīd ibn Abī ʿUbayd | 
3. Yazīd ibn Abī ʿUbayd | "from" (عن) → Salamah ibn al-Akwaʿ | Yazīd is Salamah's freed slave
4. Salamah ibn al-Akwaʿ | "(that) the Prophet ﷺ was brought a funeral..." (أن النبي ﷺ) | 

Three narrators between al-Bukhārī and the Prophet ﷺ: one of the thulāthiyyāt of al-Bukhārī.

## Narrators

### Abū ʿĀṣim al-Nabīl
- Full name and nasab: al-Ḍaḥḥāk ibn Makhlad ibn Muslim al-Shaybānī
- Kunya: Abū ʿĀṣim
- Laqab / title: al-Nabīl
- Nisba(s): al-Shaybānī (tribal); al-Baṣrī
- Mawla status: not stated
- City / region: Basra
- Tabaqa: ninth generation (Taqrīb)
- Taqrib grading line: "trustworthy and firm" [ثقة ثبت, added]
- Critics' statements: none quoted individually beyond the Taqrīb line
- Teachers / students mentioned: (students, from the sābiq/lāḥiq note) Jarīr ibn Ḥāzim and Muḥammad ibn Ḥibbān — al-Khaṭīb
- Born / died: died 212 AH or shortly after (Taqrīb); the notes add "It is also said in the forties after one hundred" (unclear in notes — possibly a birth date); born when his mother was twelve (al-Maqdisī, from ʿAmr ibn ʿAlī)
- Rumuz: all six
- Notable: known by kunya + laqab; the two students Jarīr ibn Ḥāzim and Muḥammad ibn Ḥibbān died 131 years apart (al-sābiq wa al-lāḥiq); one of al-Bukhārī's five shaykhs in the thulāthiyyāt.

### Yazīd ibn Abī ʿUbayd
- (The notes' biography section skips from "The first" to "The third"; no separate entry for him.) Facts given incidentally:
- Mawla status: freed slave (mawlā) of Salamah ibn al-Akwaʿ — "a mawla from a lower level"
- Teachers mentioned: Salamah ibn al-Akwaʿ
- Students mentioned: Abū ʿĀṣim al-Nabīl, al-Makkī ibn Ibrāhīm
- Born / died: lived "around seventy years" after Salamah, dying "around 140+ AH"
- Notable: one of the four teachers-of-teachers in al-Bukhārī's thulāthiyyāt.

### Salamah ibn al-Akwaʿ (Companion)
- Full name and nasab: Salamah ibn ʿAmr ibn al-Akwaʿ; al-Akwaʿ's name was Sinān; al-Aslamī
- Kunya: Abū Muslim; also said Abū Iyās; also Abū ʿĀmir (Tahdhīb)
- Nisba(s): al-Aslamī (tribal)
- Mawla status: patron ("a mawla from a higher level"): Yazīd ibn Abī ʿUbayd was his freed slave
- Companion facts: witnessed the pledge of al-Riḍwān; narrated from the Prophet ﷺ, Abū Bakr, ʿUmar, ʿUthmān, Ṭalḥah; brave, skilled archer, said to outrun a horse on foot
- Students mentioned: his son Iyās, his freed slave Yazīd ibn Abī ʿUbayd, others
- Hadith counts: 77 total (al-Khazrajī, al-Khulāṣah); agreed 16; al-Bukhārī alone 5; Muslim alone 9; Ibn Ḥajar (introduction to al-Fatḥ): 20 in al-Bukhārī
- Died: 74 AH
- Rumuz: all six
- Notable: the Companion of 17 of al-Bukhārī's thulāthiyyāt.

## Explanation (sharh)
- Vocabulary notes:
  - janāzah — the deceased or the bier; "ʿalayhā" feminine by form, "ʿalayhi" masculine by meaning
  - "min" before the indefinite "dayn" — emphasises generality: any debt, small or large
- Commentaries cited: Fatḥ al-Bārī (link to the bab: had retraction been possible, the Prophet would not have prayed; the Abū Qatādah identification); Ibn Baṭṭāl (why placed under Ḥawālah: guarantee and transfer both shift liability); al-Shawkānī (prefers multiple incidents to reconcile two vs three dinars); other narrations (third funeral; the later "I am closer to the believers..." ruling; the qīrāṭ hadith)
- Points stated: the Prophet asked whether wealth was left to settle the debt; his refraining was to urge settling debts in life; stronger view it was permissible, later replaced when he took on debts himself; Abū Qatādah is al-Ḥārith (also ʿAmr or al-Nuʿmān) ibn Ribʿī, Anṣārī, present at Uḥud and after
- Lessons / rulings as stated:
  1. Funeral prayer is obligatory (communal obligation).
  2. People of merit should attend funerals.
  3. The leader should enquire about people's affairs.
  4. Legitimacy of financial guarantee; permissible to take on a deceased's debt.
  5. The deceased is cleared when another pays his debt.
  6. Warning against debt except in necessity; encouragement to repay promptly.
  7. A guarantee is binding by mere commitment; the guarantor cannot withdraw.
  8. Depriving others of rights may cost one's own rights.
  9. Preventing widespread harm precedes individual benefit.
  10. Recompense corresponds to actions.
  11. The guarantor cannot reclaim from the estate if wealth later appears.
  12. The Companions' compassion for one another (Abū Qatādah).
  13. Delaying clarification until the time of need is permissible.
