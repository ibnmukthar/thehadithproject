# Authenticity audit, 23 October 2026

Before launch, every factual claim in the prototype dataset and the site copy was re-checked by four independent fact-checkers working from the raw files, not from the notes of whoever wrote them. Each was told to assume nothing was right and to look for errors, misattributions, wrong numbers and overstatements, and to report every claim with a status and the evidence.

Status key: CONFIRMED (matched against a primary text or a reliable index), PARTLY (substance right, wording, source or number imprecise), UNCONFIRMED (could not be verified in any reachable source), WRONG (contradicted by the sources). Everything marked WRONG or PARTLY was corrected in the data before this report was written; the correction is noted in each section. Items that remain UNCONFIRMED are either removed from the site or marked as such on the page.

## 1. Jami' al-Tirmidhi 2317, "Leaving what does not concern you"

Files: `src/data/hadith/leaving-what-does-not-concern.ts`, `src/data/narrators-tirmidhi.ts`. 83 claims checked.

| Status | Count |
|---|---|
| CONFIRMED | 54 |
| PARTLY | 18 |
| UNCONFIRMED | 2 |
| WRONG | 6 |

Sources used: sunnah.com (2317, 2318, Ibn Majah 3976, Muwatta 47, Nawawi 40 no. 12, Riyad al-Salihin 67, narrator pages), dorar.net's verdict index, Shamela narrator pages, Arabic Wikisource (Ibn Rajab's Jami' al-Ulum hadith 12; al-Bukhari's al-Tarikh al-Kabir; the Siyar), Tarikh Baghdad via the Hawramani index, islamtics (Musnad Ahmad 1732, 1737), A. al-Aqla's published study of the hadith's routes, zubairalizai.com and midad.com for a death date.

Errors found and corrected:

1. **Zubair Ali Za'i's death year** was given as 1434 AH; he died 10 November 2013, which is Muharram 1435. Corrected.
2. **A reply to the praise of Qurra** ("everything he narrated from al-Zuhri does not amount to sixty hadith") was attributed to Abu Hatim al-Razi. It is Ibn Hibban's, in al-Thiqat 7/342. Corrected.
3. **Al-Daraqutni's words on Qurra** were sourced to al-Dhahabi's al-Kashif; they are from Sunan al-Daraqutni 1/427, and the fuller quotation is now given. Corrected.
4. **Al-Dhahabi's description of al-Awza'i** was sourced to the Siyar; it is from al-Kashif 3/272. Corrected.
5. **A sentence in al-Awza'i's biography** said every known copy of the connected chain passes through him. At least four connected routes bypass him (through Suhayl from Abu Salih; the routes from al-Husayn ibn Ali; from Zayd ibn Thabit; from Sa'id ibn al-Musayyib). Rewritten to say that both canonical copies of the Abu Hurayra chain pass through him.
6. **A grading of Isma'il ibn Sama'a attributed to Ibn Ma'in** ("reliable; the most knowledgeable of people about al-Awza'i") could not be found in Tahdhib al-Kamal, which credits "reliable" to al-Nasa'i, al-Ijli and Ibn Ammar. Replaced with those three and the volume and page.
7. **Al-Bukhari's quoted words** included "mursalan", which is Ibn Rajab's paraphrase; al-Bukhari wrote "this is more correct for its gap … it is not sound except from Ali ibn Husayn from the Prophet ﷺ". The quotation now gives his words and the explanation gives Ibn Rajab's summary. The link, which pointed at al-Daraqutni's entry, now points at the index.
8. **Ibn Rajab's and al-Tirmidhi's quotations** had dropped the words "from the Prophet ﷺ" and joined separate passages without ellipses. Restored.
9. **Al-Zuhri's biography** claimed "eleven" versions of the hadith are attributed to him; no source gives that count. Changed to "many".
10. **Ahmad ibn Nasr's grading by al-Nasa'i** appears only in aggregator records, not in the Tahdhib al-Kamal text they reproduce. Removed pending a primary source.

Lesser corrections: the Muwatta note now names Yahya al-Laythi's recension; Abu Dawud's third "sufficing" hadith is given in his wording ("content for his brother", not "loves for his brother"); Abu Hatim's line on Abu Mushir now carries the eloquence clause in Arabic as well as English; al-Daraqutni's "reason" no longer overstates his conclusion; Isma'il ibn Sama'a's death is placed in the decade 181–190 AH; al-Zuhri's death is given as 125 with the earlier variants; the lesson "eight of the nine are of the highest rank" now says "rated reliable without dispute"; Ibn Abi Zayd's statement is marked as reported by Ibn al-Salah; al-Arna'ut's "sahih by its witnesses" in his Riyad al-Salihin takhrij is noted; Muslim's single use of Qurra is cited (1591e).

Still unconfirmed, and shown as such: nothing on this page. Two sources (Ibn Abd al-Barr's al-Tamhid 9/199 and Ibn Hajar's Ithaf al-Mahara 16/205) were confirmed only through al-Aqla's verbatim quotation of them, because neither could be opened directly; the page cites them as she does.

## 2. Site copy, the science explainer, grade bands and the blueprint

Files: `src/app/page.tsx`, `src/app/science/page.tsx`, `src/components/Conditions.tsx`, `src/components/SiteFooter.tsx`, `src/lib/bands.ts`, `docs/BLUEPRINT.md`, `README.md`. 62 claims checked.

| Status | Count |
|---|---|
| CONFIRMED | 39 |
| PARTLY | 18 |
| UNCONFIRMED | 3 |
| WRONG | 4 |

Sources used: the Taqrib's introduction and Ibn Hajar's Nuzhat al-Nazar, Ibn al-Salah's Muqaddima, al-Rihla and Jami' al-Ulum on Arabic Wikisource; sunnah.com (Bukhari 110, Muslim 3 and the introduction, Bukhari 4302, Tirmidhi 2317–2318); Shamela (Hady al-Sari via Ahmad Shakir's note, Tuhfat al-Ashraf's introduction, narrator pages for Isma'il ibn Ayyash, Hammad ibn Salama and Ma'mar); dorar.net; islamweb's fatwa on edition numbering; the Mendeley and GitHub pages of the datasets cited.

Errors found and corrected:

1. **The twelve ranks of narrators on the science page were wrong.** The list split Ibn Hajar's second rank (praise doubled or superlative) into two, which shifted ranks three to five up by one, and omitted his sixth rank (maqbul: accepted where corroborated, otherwise soft) altogether; one Arabic phrase in rank two was not Ibn Hajar's. The list was rewritten from the Taqrib's own introduction, and the summary sentence beneath it ("ranks one to three are reliable…") corrected to match. This was the most serious error found in the audit.
2. **A conflation of two grades in the blueprint.** "Sahih by its witnesses (hasan li-ghayrihi)" mixed up sahih li-ghayrihi and hasan li-ghayrihi, which Ibn Hajar distinguishes. Corrected, and the explainer's definition of hasan li-ghayrihi now says that only a light weakness can be repaired by corroboration.
3. **A wrong example in the section on numbering integrity.** The crosswalk row gave "Book 64, Hadith 325 (Khan translation)"; the in-book reference is 335, and the label was wrong. Corrected to the in-book reference and the deprecated USC-MSA reference.
4. **Sahih Muslim's Darussalam count** was given as "about 7,500"; it is 7,563. Corrected.
5. **The definition of tadlis** ("saying 'from' over people he never met") was, in Ibn Hajar's own terms, the definition of a hidden mursal. Corrected on the science page and in the blueprint; the exception for a mudallis now requires his hearing of that report, not merely of that teacher.
6. **The Arabic label for the "Unassessed" band was majhul**, which is itself one of Ibn Hajar's verdicts (rank nine). Replaced with "ghayr muqayyam" and the band's description now says it is a state of our data, not a verdict.
7. **"Lose one of the first three conditions and the report is weak"** understated Ibn al-Salah's definition, which also excludes the anomalous and the defective. Changed to "fail any one of the five".
8. **The Taqrib's scope** was described as the six canonical collections; it covers the six and the works appended to them (Muslim's introduction, Abu Dawud's Marasil, al-Tirmidhi's Shama'il, al-Nasa'i's Amal al-Yawm wa al-Layla and others). Corrected on both pages and in the blueprint.

Lesser corrections: the hero caption now says seven people "al-Bukhari included" stand between the reader and the Prophet ﷺ (six narrators plus the compiler); the comparison with Herodotus is stated precisely ("almost never names the people who told him"); the claim that we know every narrator's death year and biography is softened to "nearly all, roughly"; al-Bukhari's condition of a proven meeting is attributed to the scholars who inferred it (al-Qadi Iyad, Ibn Rajab, Ibn Hajar) and limited to ambiguous formulae, as it should be; Ibn Sirin's statement is cited to narration 26 of Muslim's introduction; the Bukhari numbering is described as printed in the standard edition of Fath al-Bari rather than "used by Ibn Hajar's commentary" (Ibn Hajar numbered nothing); the letter suffixes for Muslim are credited to sunnah.com's convention; a taraf is a tell-tale fragment, not necessarily the opening; Tuhfat al-Ashraf's scope includes the appended works; the landing page now calls the Tirmidhi example "disputed" rather than "hasan", matching its page; the README names the two published studies.

Confirmed without change, among others: 2,602 as Ibn Hajar's count of al-Bukhari's connected hadith without repetition; al-Bukhari's own statement that he drew the book from some six hundred thousand reports; the footer hadith's citations (Bukhari 110, Muslim 3); its status as mass-transmitted; Ibn Sirin's statement and its translation; the five conditions and their Arabic terms; Ibn al-Salah's definition and death year; the critics named; the five ranks of mudallisin and the placement of Yahya ibn Sa'id (first) and Sufyan ibn Uyaynah (second); the three examples of scoped reliability (Isma'il ibn Ayyash, Hammad ibn Salama, Ma'mar); the definitions of corroboration; the uprightness of the Companions; the numbering example 4051/4302; the dataset licences.

Still unconfirmed: nothing on the pages. One blueprint detail (the Multi-IsnadSet's narrator-id column) is marked for verification when the data is actually loaded.

## 3. Sahih al-Bukhari 1, "Actions are by intentions"

Files: `src/data/hadith/actions-by-intentions.ts`, `src/data/narrators.ts`. 74 claims checked.

| Status | Count |
|---|---|
| CONFIRMED | 55 |
| PARTLY | 10 |
| UNCONFIRMED | 1 |
| WRONG | 8 |

Sources used: sunnah.com (Bukhari 1, 54, 2529, 3898, 5070, 6689, 6953; Muslim 1907a–b; Abu Dawud 2201; Tirmidhi 1647; Nasa'i 75; Ibn Majah 4227; Nawawi 40 no. 1), Fath al-Bari on hadith 1, Hady al-Sari, Jami' al-Ulum wa al-Hikam hadith 1, Majmu' al-Fatawa vol. 18 and the Taqrib (entries 2451, 3320, 4685, 5691, 5727, 7559) on Arabic Wikisource; Shamela narrator records and al-Ightibat no. 44, the preface of Sahih Ibn Hibban (al-Ihsan 1/161) and al-Nawawi's commentary on Muslim 13/53; an OCR copy of Ibn Hajar's Tabaqat al-Mudallisin for the entry sequence.

Errors found and corrected:

1. **Muslim 1907b** was said to add "seven" further routes from Yahya ibn Sa'id; it adds eight, now listed by name.
2. **Al-Nawawi's grade** was quoted as "agreed upon as sound", which is Ibn Hajar's and Ibn Taymiyya's phrase, not al-Nawawi's. His own words, from his commentary on Muslim, are now quoted ("the Muslims agree on the great standing of this hadith, its many benefits and its soundness"), and the Forty's note that the two imams recorded it in the soundest books composed.
3. **"Later scholars called it a third of knowledge"** was backwards: the early imams did, al-Shafi'i, Ibn Mahdi and Ahmad among them (Ibn Hajar lists eight). Corrected.
4. **Ibn Hibban's statement on Sufyan's tadlis** was sourced to al-Thiqat; it is from the preface to his Sahih (al-Ihsan 1/161), and the fuller sentence is now given.
5. **Al-Dhahabi's reason for rejecting the report of Sufyan's late confusion** was paraphrased as "al-Qattan died before the date given". Al-Qattan died in Safar 198, after the date, but as the pilgrims were only just returning with news from the Hijaz, leaving no time to learn of it and testify. Corrected, and Ibn Hajar's acceptance of the report is now noted beside al-Dhahabi's rejection.
6. **A clause claiming al-Bukhari "preferred the version with explicit hearing"** has no support in Fath al-Bari, which only records the recension difference. Deleted.
7. **A source link labelled as Ibn Hajar's Tabaqat al-Mudallisin** pointed to a modern summary of it by Hammad al-Ansari. Relabelled.
8. **Yahya ibn Sa'id al-Ansari** was placed in "the generation after the Successors"; he is a junior Successor who met Anas ibn Malik. Corrected.

Lesser corrections: the seven places in al-Bukhari run through five of Yahya's students, not an unspecified number; Ibn Mahdi said the hadith should be placed "in every chapter" (the "head of every chapter" wording is Ibn Hajar's); "the most accepted hadith in the corpus" is softened to "one of the most universally accepted"; the lesson on Sufyan's formula now says "in the standard recension"; al-Bukhari's conditions are described as inferred by later scholars rather than stated by him, and 2,602 is specified as connected hadith without repetition (2,761 with the suspended reports), on the landing page too; Abu Hatim's two separate reports on al-Humaydi are quoted separately rather than fused; Ahmad's remark on Muhammad ibn Ibrahim is quoted in full ("objectionable reports, or reports that are objectionable"); the Taqrib line on Alqama restores "of the second generation", his kunya is added, and the dispute over his Companion status is stated with Ibn Hajar's own hesitation; the definition of mashhur is corrected to the technical one (three or more narrators at every level) on both pages that use it; the Umm Qays report no longer says "to Madinah" (the report does not) and Ibn Rajab's caution about it is added; Umar's birth is marked as the usual estimate.

Confirmed without change, among others: the Arabic chain and text, the transmission formulae, all twelve parallel narrations and their chapters, al-Tirmidhi's exact words, Ibn Hajar's and Ibn Taymiyya's and Ibn Rajab's quotations, the counts of narrators from Yahya (250, more than 300, 700, and Ibn Hajar's doubt), the named students, every Taqrib entry and number, the critics' words on each narrator, Sufyan's second rank and entry 52 among the mudallisin, Yahya's first rank, and the abridgement of hadith 1 relative to the other six.

Still unconfirmed: nothing on the page.

## 4. "Seek knowledge even in China" (al-Khatib, al-Rihla 1)

Files: `src/data/hadith/seek-knowledge-even-in-china.ts`, `src/data/narrators-khatib.ts`. 64 claims checked.

| Status | Count |
|---|---|
| CONFIRMED | 43 |
| PARTLY | 12 |
| UNCONFIRMED | 2 |
| WRONG | 7 |

Sources used: al-Rihla no. 1 with its editor's footnotes, al-Majruhin (two editions), al-Du'afa al-Kabir, Mizan al-I'tidal, al-Mughni, al-Kashif, Tahdhib al-Tahdhib, al-Jarh wa al-Ta'dil, Su'alat al-Hakim, Musnad al-Bazzar, Fayd al-Qadir, Tadrib al-Rawi, Ibn al-Salah's Muqaddima, Jami' Bayan al-Ilm, al-Maqasid al-Hasana, al-Mawdu'at, al-Silsila al-Da'ifa, Hidayat al-Ruwat, al-Madkhal and Tarikh Baghdad on Shamela; the Taqrib on Wikisource; the Siyar via tarajm.com; dorar.net and sunnah.com.

Errors found and corrected:

1. **A "consensus on his weakness" line attributed to Ibn Abd al-Barr about Abu Atika** concerns a different narrator of the same name, Tarif ibn Shihab; the book's editor had conflated the two. Deleted, and replaced with al-Dhahabi's actual words on Abu Atika in al-Mughni ("his name is disputed; his weakness is agreed upon").
2. **A grading of al-Hasan ibn Ali ibn Affan credited to Abu Hatim al-Razi** is his son Ibn Abi Hatim's ("we wrote from him; he is truthful"). Corrected with the volume and page. Al-Hakim's "reliable" was sourced to the Su'alat, which records al-Daraqutni's answers; it is from al-Mustadrak. Corrected.
3. **Al-Hasan ibn Atiyya's generation** was given as the tenth; the Taqrib places him in the ninth. Corrected, with the entry number.
4. **The criticism of al-Tarrazi's father** was attributed to al-Hakim; it is al-Khatib's own, in Tarikh Baghdad, and says nothing about "late in life". Corrected.
5. **Al-Asamm's deafness** was described as coming "in later life"; the Siyar says it struck him in his twenties after his travels and later became total. Corrected.
6. **"Entries from 62 biographical dictionaries"** overstated the Hawramani page, which draws on about ten works for this narrator. Corrected.
7. **Ibn al-Jawzi's grade** was rendered as "fabricated"; his words are "this hadith is not sound", and the placement in al-Mawdu'at is the judgement. Corrected, with the note that he also faulted al-Hasan ibn Atiyya while misquoting Abu Hatim.

Lesser corrections: "the only chain" is now "the only chain that does not run through a known liar or fabricator", since Ibn Abd al-Barr and Ibn Adi record two further routes through such narrators; Ibn Adi's entry now carries only his own words (the phrase "objectionable in hadith" there is al-Bukhari's, which Ibn Adi quotes); al-Daraqutni's "weak" is marked as reported by al-Mizzi, his own book merely listing the narrator; the Taqrib's own line on Abu Atika ("weak; al-Sulaymani went further") and his generation are added; "al-Bukhari's harshest formula" is softened to "one of his severest"; the claim that Anas's name was "the one most often borrowed" by weak narrators is softened to "many later claimed to have heard him"; al-Sakhawi's supposed acceptance of the obligation sentence, which could not be verified, is replaced by al-Iraqi's report that some imams graded its routes sound; al-Sakhawi's entry number (126) and his actual words are given; page references that followed other editions are corrected (al-Bazzar 1/172–175 no. 95, with his spelling of the kunya; Fayd al-Qadir 4/267 no. 5264; Tadrib al-Rawi by section; Jami' Bayan al-Ilm by entry number in al-Zuhayri's edition) and the links now point at the sources rather than at the al-Rihla footnote; Ibn Abd al-Barr's verdict is scoped to every route from Anas, as his chapter heading has it; al-Tirmidhi's single hadith through al-Hasan ibn Atiyya and Abu Atika is identified (726).

Confirmed without change, among others: the chain and text of al-Rihla no. 1 and its formulae; the editor's own grade of "weak"; al-Uqayli's, Ibn Hibban's, al-Bayhaqi's and al-Albani's words and loci; Ahmad's "nothing is established with us" on the obligation sentence; al-Mizzi's and al-Suyuti's acceptance of it by its routes, and al-Suyuti's fifty routes; Ibn al-Salah's use of it as the example of a famous but unsound report; al-Sakhawi's warning about "and every Muslim woman"; al-Bukhari's rule that a narrator he calls "objectionable in hadith" may not be narrated from; the dates of al-Khatib, al-Asamm, Ibn Affan and Anas; al-Dhahabi's description of al-Asamm; al-Dhahabi's remark that Abu Atika is "the author of" this hadith.

Still unconfirmed, and left out of the page: nothing.

Every item in the Partly and Wrong columns was corrected before publication. Of the eight Unconfirmed items, those on the pages were removed or reworded so that the page no longer asserts them; the rest are notes in the blueprint marked for verification when the relevant data is loaded.

What the audit says about the method: none of the errors was in a hadith text, a chain, a transmission formula, a Taqrib line or an entry number. They were in the layer around the record: which critic said a thing, which book it is in, which edition's page number, how far a generalisation can be pushed. That is exactly the layer the project's data model is built to pin down (critic, work, locus, scope, verbatim words), and it is why the production dataset must be entered from the books with a page reference on every line rather than from summaries, however good.
